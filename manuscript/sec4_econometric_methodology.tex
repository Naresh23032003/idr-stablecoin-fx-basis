\section{Econometric methodology}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Two-leg basis decomposition and external peg wedge}
For cryptocurrency $X \in \{\mathrm{BTC}, \mathrm{ETH}, \mathrm{XRP}\}$ at synchronized time $t$, denote the domestic rupiah price on Indodax by $P_{X,t}^{IDR}$, the global Tether-quoted price on Binance by $P_{X,t}^{USDT}$, the domestic stablecoin exchange rate on Indodax by $U_t^{IDR/USDT}$, and the Bank Indonesia JISDOR USD/IDR fixing by $S_t^{IDR/USD}$. Defining log deviations in basis points ($\times 10^4$), we decompose the total basis relative to official USD/IDR into two economic legs:
\begin{align}
  B_t^{total} &= 10^4 \left( \ln P_{X,t}^{IDR} - \ln P_{X,t}^{USDT} - \ln S_t^{IDR/USD} \right), \label{eq:btot} \\
  B_t^{crypto} &= 10^4 \left( \ln P_{X,t}^{IDR} - \ln P_{X,t}^{USDT} - \ln U_t^{IDR/USDT} \right), \label{eq:bcry} \\
  B_t^{stablecoin\text{-}FX} &= 10^4 \left( \ln U_t^{IDR/USDT} - \ln S_t^{IDR/USD} \right), \label{eq:bsf}
\end{align}
so that $B_t^{total} \equiv B_t^{crypto} + B_t^{stablecoin\text{-}FX}$ holds identically at every date. Because USD and USDT can trade at a small spread, we separately track the external USD/USDT peg wedge $W_t^{USD/USDT} = 10^4 \ln P_t^{USDT/USD}$ from Coinbase \texttt{USDT-USD} so that dollar-denominated pricing deviations are measured rather than assumed away. By bilinearity of covariance, total basis variance decomposes exactly as
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{Var}(B_t^{total}) = \mathrm{Var}(B_t^{crypto}) + \mathrm{Var}(B_t^{stablecoin\text{-}FX}) + 2\,\mathrm{Cov}(B_t^{crypto}, B_t^{stablecoin\text{-}FX}).
  \label{eq:vardecomp}
\end{equation}
We verify numerically that discrepancy across the identity is below $10^{-10}\text{ bps}^2$. Because Equation~\eqref{eq:vardecomp} is an accounting identity, the variance shares describe which leg moves and do not identify a causal shock.

\subsection{Unit roots, cointegration tests, and block-wild bootstrap}
Because the rupiah exchange rate is $I(1)$, pairing rupiah-quoted bitcoin with unconverted USDT quotes inherits the exchange-rate unit root by construction; currency conversion is therefore necessary before testing cross-venue cointegration. After checking integration order with Augmented Dickey--Fuller (ADF), Phillips--Perron (PP) \citep{phillips1988testing}, KPSS \citep{kwiatkowski1992testing}, and single-break Zivot--Andrews \citep{zivot1992further} tests, we estimate three cointegration procedures (bootstrap $p$-values use $B = 9{,}999$ draws in the daily baseline, so the smallest reportable value is $1/(B+1) = 0.0001$):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Engle--Granger (1987) two-step test:} Regresses $\ln P_{X,t}^{IDR} = a + \beta \ln P_{X,t}^{*} + e_t$ with OLS, then tests $\hat{e}_t$ using MacKinnon (1996) residual response-surface critical values \citep{engle1987cointegration,mackinnon1996numerical}.
  \item \textbf{Fixed-$(1,-1)$ spread stationarity test:} Evaluates ADF and KPSS statistics directly on the imposed log spread $\ln P_{X,t}^{IDR} - \ln P_{X,t}^{*}$ without estimating $\beta$.
  \item \textbf{Johansen maximum likelihood with finite-sample corrections:} Evaluates Trace and Max-Eigenvalue statistics with an unrestricted constant ($H_1(r)$, \texttt{det\_order=0}, one lagged difference in the daily baseline), allowing linear drift in log asset/FX prices alongside a non-zero mean in the cointegrating spread. Both Reinsel--Ahn (1992) statistic scaling $\frac{T - kp}{T}\lambda_{LR}$ and Cheung--Lai (1993) critical-value scaling $\frac{T}{T - kp} c_{\infty}$ are reported \citep{johansen1991estimation,reinsel1992vector,cheung1993finite}.
  \item \textbf{Sequential null-imposed restricted VECM block-wild bootstrap:} Because high-frequency and stablecoin--FX residuals exhibit conditional heteroskedasticity (ARCH clustering) and serial correlation (Ljung--Box and ARCH $p < 0.001$), an i.i.d.\ residual bootstrap is misspecified. Following \citet{cavaliere2010testing} and \citet{cavaliere2012bootstrap} for heteroskedastic VARs and \citet{paparoditis2003residual} for block-based unit-root/cointegration resampling under short-range serial dependence, we test both $H_0: \mathrm{rank}(\Pi) = 0$ (using the restricted VAR in first differences) and $H_0: \mathrm{rank}(\Pi) \le r_0$ for $r_0 \ge 1$ (using the ML-estimated rank-$r_0$ VECM). Centered residual vectors $\hat{u}_t$ are multiplied by block-level Rademacher wild weights $w_m \in \{-1, +1\}$ (constant within consecutive blocks of length $b = \mathrm{round}(T_{eff}^{1/4})$, so $b = 6$ for $T_{eff} = 1,412$ and $b = 28$ for $T_{eff} = 616,677$), preserving contemporaneous cross-market covariance $\Sigma_u$, ARCH/GARCH clustering, and within-block serial correlation. We validate empirical size and power with $n_{MC} = 1,000$ Monte Carlo replications under GARCH(1,1) and AR(1) innovations for $T \in \{25, 100, 500\}$, plus sensitivity across block lengths, AR persistence, and Student-$t_5$ innovations.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{VECM adjustment, price discovery, threshold models, and event windows}
For cointegrated pairs $Y_t = [\ln P_{X,t}^{IDR},\, \ln P_{X,t}^{*}]'$ at daily frequency, we estimate the bivariate VECM with an unrestricted constant ($H_1(r)$, \texttt{co}):
\begin{equation}
  \Delta Y_t = \mu_0 + \alpha \beta' Y_{t-1} + \sum_{j=1}^{p-1} \Gamma_j \Delta Y_{t-j} + u_t,
  \label{eq:vecm}
\end{equation}
and report both ML standard errors and Newey--West HAC standard errors ($L=5$ daily, $L=12$ five-minute) for adjustment coefficients \citep{newey1987simple}. For the continuous 24/7 five-minute BTC archive, converting Binance \texttt{BTC/USDT} into rupiah via synchronous Indodax \texttt{USDT/IDR} would embed a domestic Indodax quote inside the synthetic global series and contaminate cross-market price-discovery weights. We therefore estimate a three-variable five-minute VECM on the regular clock-time grid ($T_{eff} = 616,677$ five-minute bars, five lagged differences, HAC $L = 12$) using $Y_t = [\ln P_{BTC,t}^{IDR},\, \ln P_{BTC,t}^{USDT},\, \ln U_t^{IDR/USDT}]'$, which separates domestic \texttt{BTC/IDR}, global Binance \texttt{BTC/USDT}, and domestic \texttt{USDT/IDR}. As sensitivity checks, we also report the irregular active-volume subsample ($605,150$ bars) and an independent-FX specification over the final 349-day overlap (November 29, 2025--November 12, 2026) that replaces Indodax \texttt{USDT/IDR} with Binance's native \texttt{USDT/IDR}.

In any VECM, the persistence of the cointegrating error $z_t = \beta' Y_t$ depends on the full adjustment vector $\alpha$, the cointegrating vector $\beta$, and the short-run lag matrices $\Gamma_j$, not on $\alpha_{local}$ alone. Rewriting Equation~\eqref{eq:vecm} in levels VAR($p$) companion form with companion matrix $F$, we compute the full-system asymptotic half-life in native sampling units as $-\ln(2)/\ln|\lambda_{\max}|$, where $|\lambda_{\max}|$ is the largest non-unit eigenvalue modulus of $F$ (excluding the $n - r$ unit roots). Because $|\lambda_{\max}|$ governs the slowest stable mode of $F$, we also inspect the remaining non-unit eigenvalues to verify whether a spectral gap separates the cointegrating mode from transient lag dynamics. In the no-lag case ($p=1$), error-correction persistence reduces to the scalar root $1 + \beta'\alpha$; we report both the full-system companion half-life (primary) and the scalar $1 + \beta'\alpha$ benchmark in native sampling units (business days for daily panels, five-minute bars for regular intraday panels). For daily return dynamics, rather than regressing $\Delta \ln P_t^{IDR}$ on contemporaneous return components that hold by accounting identity, we report the non-mechanical one-day lagged global return catch-up coefficient.

Following \citet{hasbrouck1995one}, in the three-variable five-minute system ($n = 3$, $r = 1$, two common stochastic trends) we report each market's share of total error-correction adjustment $|\alpha_i|/\sum_{j=1}^3 |\alpha_j|$, weak-exogeneity tests for all three equations, and 6-permutation Cholesky Hasbrouck Information Share bounds for the permanent component of $\ln P_{BTC,t}^{IDR}$ derived from the first row of the Granger permanent-impact matrix $\Psi(1) = \beta_\perp(\alpha_\perp' \Gamma \beta_\perp)^{-1}\alpha_\perp'$, where $\Gamma = I - \sum_{j=1}^{p-1}\Gamma_j$. Because daily contemporaneous residual correlation exceeds $0.99$ and several daily bivariate systems exhibit same-sign point estimates for $\alpha_{local}$ and $\alpha_{global}$, we do not report daily Gonzalo--Granger weights and instead rely on HAC weak-exogeneity tests at daily frequency.

To test transaction-cost band nonlinearity, we estimate a symmetric three-regime Band-TAR model on basis $B_t$ that nests the linear $\mathrm{AR}(p+1)$ model under $H_0$:
\begin{multline}
  \Delta B_t = c_{in}\mathbb{I}_{|B_{t-1}|\le\tau} + \rho_{in}(B_{t-1}\mathbb{I}_{|B_{t-1}|\le\tau}) + c_+\mathbb{I}_{B_{t-1}>\tau} + c_-\mathbb{I}_{B_{t-1}<-\tau} \\
  + \rho_{out}z_{out,t-1} + \sum_{j=1}^{p}\phi_j\Delta B_{t-j} + \varepsilon_t,
  \label{eq:tar}
\end{multline}
where $z_{out,t-1} = (B_{t-1} - \mathrm{sign}(B_{t-1})\tau)\mathbb{I}_{|B_{t-1}|>\tau}$. Grid search across the middle $70\%$ of $|B_t|$ yields the $\sup W(\tau)$ statistic, evaluated by linear-AR residual bootstrap \citep{hansen2002testing}. For the ten documented institutional and regulatory milestones, our primary inference uses $\pm 30$-calendar-day OLS regressions on each BTC basis leg ($B_t^{crypto}$ and $B_t^{stablecoin\text{-}FX}$) with a post-event indicator, controlling for daily global BTC log returns and daily JISDOR log returns, with Newey--West HAC standard errors ($L=5$). Unadjusted two-sided Welch $t$-tests and Mann--Whitney $U$ tests are reported alongside as secondary checks. Because three event pairs overlap within $\pm 30$ calendar days and the October 31, 2026 event has only 12 post-event calendar days before sample end, we also estimate a joint non-overlapping $\pm 15$-day multi-event regression across all ten events simultaneously and an overlap-excluded check for the November 1, 2024 CFX launch. Holm multiple-testing adjustment is applied across the 20-test family (10 events $\times$ 2 legs) within the primary BTC daily panel.
