\subsection{Five-decimal reconciliation of October 2026 Indodax Pro rates}
Under PMK No.\ 50 Tahun 2025 (effective August 1, 2025) \citep{pmk50_2025}, PERBA No.\ 9/2024 \citep{perba9_2024}, and the two-stage CFX/KKI fee halvings under SEB No.\ 001/SEB/CFX-KKI/SPOT/II/2026 (March 1, 2026) and the October 1, 2026 Stage-2 cut \citep{cfx_seb001_2026}, base bourse-plus-clearing levies fell from $2.00\text{ bps}$ (IDR) and $4.00\text{ bps}$ (USDT) in Regime R3 to $1.00/2.00\text{ bps}$ in R5 and $0.50/1.00\text{ bps}$ in R6. Applying $11\%$ service VAT ($\times 1.11$) yields exact VAT-inclusive CFX/KKI pass-through charges of $2.22/4.44\text{ bps}$ (R3--R4), $1.11/2.22\text{ bps}$ (R5), and $0.555/1.11\text{ bps}$ (R6). Published October 2026 Indodax Pro rates \citep{indodax_fees_2026} therefore equal $10.555\text{ bps}$ ($0.10555\%$) maker and $20.555\text{ bps}$ ($0.20555\%$) taker on IDR buy orders ($10.0/20.0\text{ bps}$ VAT-inclusive platform commission plus $0\text{ bps}$ buyer VAT plus $0.555\text{ bps}$ CFX/KKI levy), and $31.555\text{ bps}$ ($0.31555\%$) maker and $41.555\text{ bps}$ ($0.41555\%$) taker on IDR sell orders ($10.0/20.0 + 21.0 + 0.555$). Route A domestic two-leg round-trip costs therefore equal $42.110\text{ bps}$ maker ($10.555 + 31.555$) and $62.110\text{ bps}$ taker ($20.555 + 41.555$). On Indodax USDT-quoted pairs, base $3.0/6.0\text{ bps}$ plus $1.11\text{ bps}$ CFX/KKI USDT levy plus $21.0\text{ bps}$ seller PPh 22 equals $25.11\text{ bps}$ ($0.2511\%$) maker and $28.11\text{ bps}$ ($0.2811\%$) taker on sell orders, matching live exchange disclosures exactly.

\subsection{Monte Carlo size and power validation of the block-wild bootstrap}
Table~\ref{tab:boot_mc} reports $n_{MC} = 1,000$ Monte Carlo replications ($B = 299$ bootstrap draws per trial, zero failed replications) under a controlled DGP with GARCH(1,1) conditional heteroskedasticity and AR(1) serial correlation ($\phi = 0.20$, contemporaneous innovation correlation $0.944$). Asymptotic and Reinsel--Ahn trace tests over-reject true $r=0$ series across all sample sizes ($13.3\%$ and $13.0\%$ even at $T = 500$). By contrast, the block-wild bootstrap substantially reduces size distortion: empirical size is $5.9\% \pm 0.8\%$ at $T=25$, modestly above nominal at $7.1\% \pm 0.8\%$ for $T=100$, and $4.7\% \pm 0.7\%$ at $T=500$. Finite-sample power under $r=1$ is low in very short samples ($8.7\% \pm 0.9\%$ at $T=25$), rising to $72.8\% \pm 1.4\%$ at $T=100$ and $100.0\%$ at $T=500$, a sample well below our empirical $T_{eff} = 1,412$. Additional sensitivity runs ($n_{MC}=200$) across $\phi \in \{0.0, 0.50\}$, Student-$t_5$ innovations, and block length $b=1$ confirm empirical size within $[4.5\%, 7.5\%]$ and $100.0\%$ power at $T=500$.

\subsection{Direct Binance IDR spot cross-validation (November 2025--November 2026)}
Over the 349-day overlap since Binance introduced direct rupiah pairs on November 29, 2025 ($N = 100,362$ five-minute bars through November 12, 2026), Indodax \texttt{BTC/IDR} averages $-0.19\text{ bps}$ (SD $53.54\text{ bps}$) relative to Binance \texttt{BTC/IDR}, and Indodax \texttt{USDT/IDR} averages $-2.11\text{ bps}$ (SD $25.82\text{ bps}$) relative to Binance \texttt{USDT/IDR}. Because Binance \texttt{BTC/IDR} is less liquid at $\$50\text{k}$ size in the November 13, 2026 snapshot ($297.38\text{ bps}$ spread vs.\ $17.99\text{ bps}$ on Indodax), domestic rupiah liquidity on that snapshot date remains concentrated on Indodax.
