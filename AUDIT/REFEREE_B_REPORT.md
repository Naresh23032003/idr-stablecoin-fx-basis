# Independent Referee B Report (Financial Economist)

**Focus:** Economic motivation, institutional setting, venue coverage, stablecoin/FX interpretation, arbitrage claims, transaction costs, regulatory event interpretation, and external validity.

## 1. Initial Major Concerns (Before Revision)
1. **Venue Coverage Overreach:** Long-horizon (2021–2025) claims rely on Indodax alone, as Tokocrypto, Pintu, and Reku lacked multi-year historical candle APIs.
2. **Fee Reconstruction Uncertainty:** May 2022–October 2024 Indodax Pro USDT fee tiers are only partially preserved in public archives (`PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`).
3. **CFX Causal Framing Risk:** Presenting $+17.37$ bps (`+18.19` bps rebuilt) on $B_t^{stablecoin\text{-}FX}$ as a regulatory effect without placebo falsification risks overclaiming.
4. **Snapshot Depth vs. Historical Liquidity:** November 13, 2026 order-book snapshot cannot establish 2021–2026 executable arbitrage profitability.

## 2. Assessment of Final Revised Version
- **Resolved:** Abstract, Introduction, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion explicitly scope pre-November 2025 evidence to **Indodax** (with 349-day Binance IDR cross-checks); JISDOR is clearly distinguished as a non-executable reference fixing; fee-band containment (`98.30%`, bounded `[97.24%, 98.59%]`) is explicitly separated from executable arbitrage; November 13, 2026 depth slippage is strictly labeled point-in-time; CFX Nov 2024 shift is explicitly qualified as a non-causal post-event association that fails placebo-date checks.
- **Remaining Scientific Limitations:** Single-venue pre-2025 coverage and missing historical L2 depth are inherent data constraints that are now transparently disclosed rather than hidden.

**Referee B Recommendation:** **Acceptable for Submission** (economic claims match what the descriptive evidence establishes).
